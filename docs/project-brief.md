# RETIVAA — Project Brief

## 1. Project Overview & Vision
**RETIVAA** is a premium desert camp retreat located in the mystical Thar Desert of Jaisalmer, Rajasthan, India. The project represents a refined, culturally grounded hospitality experience designed for discerning travelers seeking tranquility, understated luxury, and authentic desert heritage without the garish or commercial tropes common to generic desert resorts.

The website serves as an evocative digital front door that mirrors the sanctuary of the dunes: tactile, serene, spacious, and grounded in Rajasthani craftsmanship.

---

## 2. Brand Identity & Location

- **Brand Name**: RETIVAA
- **Location**: Jaisalmer, Rajasthan, India
- **Core Geography**: The Great Thar Desert / Sam Sand Dunes
- **Brand Tagline**: *Where the dunes become your home.*
- **Supporting Narrative**: *Tent stays, desert safaris and evenings filled with the spirit of Rajasthan.*

---

## 3. Confirmed Camp Facilities
To maintain strict marketing integrity, the website only promotes verified, confirmed camp offerings:

1. **Accommodation in Tent**: Premium handcrafted tent architecture nestled among the dunes.
2. **Camel Safari in Thar Desert**: Traditional sunset caravan journeys through untouched ridges.
3. **Sunset Point in Sand Dunes**: Dedicated panoramic viewpoint for desert dusks.
4. **Evening Hi-Tea Snacks**: Traditional Rajasthani savories and tea at golden hour.
5. **Rajasthani Folk Dance**: Authentic Kalbelia and Ghoomar performances by hereditary artists.
6. **Live Rajasthani Folk Music**: Soulful desert ragas featuring the kamaicha, dholak, and harmonium.
7. **Camp Fire – Fire Show**: Mesmerizing desert fire performances under the starlit sky.
8. **Dinner & Breakfast**: Authentic regional culinary hospitality.
9. **Jeep Safari**: Thrilling dune-bashing expeditions across expansive sands.

*Strict Architectural Rule*: No speculative amenities, fabricated package rates, false accolades, or unverified activity schedules may be published without client authorization.

---

## 4. Visual & Aesthetic Inspiration
The design aesthetic balances desert warmth with editorial high-fashion restraint:

- **Era Residence** ([https://www.era-residence.com/](https://www.era-residence.com/)): Inspiration for typography scale, generous whitespace, architectural discipline, and serene transitions.
- **White Desert** ([https://white-desert.com/](https://white-desert.com/)): Inspiration for extreme landscape photography, remote expedition luxury, and quiet elegance.

*Important Note*: These sites serve solely as atmospheric benchmarks. All branding, layout code, typography choices, and content for RETIVAA are strictly custom-built. No assets or text are copied.

---

## 5. Primary Guest Flow
1. **Curtain Arrival**: An evocative curtain parting intro that transitions the visitor from the digital realm into the secluded world of the camp.
2. **Landscape & Stay Discovery**: Immersive editorial storytelling covering tented accommodation, desert safaris, and cultural evenings.
3. **Curated Visual Gallery**: Category-filtered photography with accessible lightbox.
4. **Direct WhatsApp Booking Enquiry**: Frictionless, direct concierge enquiry channel connecting prospective guests directly to the retreat's reservations team on WhatsApp without deceptive booking engines or fake payments.
